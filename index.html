PixLite: Privacy Policy

Effective: 17 July 2026 · Last updated: 17 July 2026

This policy applies to the PixLite Android application (package com.photocompressor.app). PixLite compresses, resizes, and converts photos. The most important thing to know: your photos are processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded to us or anyone else.

Data we access

When you pick photos, you do so through Android's own system photo picker, so PixLite only ever sees the specific images you select: it does not browse or index your photo library. Selected images are read only to produce the output you asked for, which is saved back to your device's gallery (the Pictures/PixLite folder). By default, GPS location tags are removed from every output; you can choose to keep them via a setting, which only affects the file saved on your own device.

Data we collect and share

PixLite's own code collects no personal information, has no account or login, and contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDK. We never receive, transmit, or store your photos. A photo leaves your device only if you explicitly share it using the Share button and Android's own share sheet.

PixLite does display ads (see Third-party services below). To serve those ads, Google collects and shares certain technical data: this is the only data collection associated with the app.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state: used only to request and display ads.
  • Photos: you select images through the system photo picker; the app only sees what you choose.
  • Notifications: used to show progress while a batch of photos is processed.
  • Media location: only used if you opt to keep GPS tags in your output.

Third-party services

PixLite shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use a device advertising identifier, IP address, and general device information. This is used for advertising only; PixLite does not share your photos or any personal data with Google. Google's handling of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Consent (EEA / UK): where required, PixLite asks for your consent before serving personalized ads, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can review or change your choice at any time from Settings → Manage ad consent inside the app. You can also reset or delete your advertising identifier in your Android system settings.

Data retention and deletion

We store none of your data on any server, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Output images live only on your device and can be deleted like any other photo: including from within PixLite's History screen.

Children

PixLite is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
